
## Summary of the Game Objectives

The purpose of The Brewing Game is to help year 2 and 3 engineering students understand the importance of instruments in industrial processes.

Through an interactive scenario, players will perform simulated brewing tasks both with and without proper instruments, experiencing the consequences of inaccuracy and appreciating the benefits of proper instruments being used.

The goal is to improve engagement with the subject and offer a practical demonstration of theoretical concepts through gamification.

# Task Analysis User 1

## Goal 1

 Understand the challenge of not using instrument

### Current Method(s)

Offline: Books, module learning resources.

Online: Research, online articles, YouTube video.

## Task 1

 Produce first batch of beer.

### Proposed methods of carrying out task

The player will be given the task of brewing beer, specifically to accomplish the brewing step of adding the correct amount of water to a mash tun without no instrument to help appreciate the level.

Task flow:

1.       Game launch

2.       Click start game

3.       Go through intro and tutorial

4.       Select mash tun

5.       Click on valve icon to start water flow

6.       Wait the amount of time they deem correct

7.       Click on valve icon to stop water flow

8.       Press on brew button to complete brewing

9.       Click on mash tun to collect batch

## Goal 2

Understand complexity of handling repeated processes in industrial settings without instrumentation.

### Current Method(s)

Offline: Books, module learning resources.

Online: Research, online articles, YouTube video.

## Task 2

 Produce enough beer to access mash tun upgrade

### Proposed methods of carrying out task

By carrying out repeated operations the user will appreciate why instruments are often used to optimise tasks

Task Flow:

Repeat first task for 3 times.

Appreciate value of using instrumental aid in carrying out industrial process.

## Task 3

Upgrade tun and produce last beer batch.

### Proposed methods of carrying out task

Once the user has created 3 batched they will be able to upgrade the mash tun to utilise a sensor level. Once they repeat the brewing operation they will be able to see the level of the water and fill at the correct level without having to guess each time.
