**1. Introduction**

1.1. Purpose of this Document

The purpose of this document is to introduce team members to the project
as well as to provide a set of streamlined guidelines to facilitate the
development environment setup.

1.2. What is The Brewing Game?

The Brewing Game is an educational game aimed at teaching the importance
of instrumentation.

**8. Onboarding Session**

Once a new team member has setup their development environment and has
familiarised themselves with the project, they will be invited to attend
an online onboarding session. The purpose of the onboarding session is
to ensure new team members have their development environment set up
correctly and understand the project's tools and workflows, during this
session new members will have an opportunity to ask questions so they
can begin contributing more confidently and effectively.
